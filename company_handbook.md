# Acme Cloud Handbook

## Refund policy
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are prorated
for annual plans only. Monthly plans are not refundable after the first 30 days.

## Support hours
Standard support: Monday-Friday, 08:00-18:00 SAST. Premium customers get 24/7 support with a
1-hour response SLA. Standard SLA is 8 business hours.

## Pricing
Starter: $29/month. Team: $99/month for up to 10 seats, $8 per additional seat. Enterprise: custom.
Annual billing gives a 20% discount on all plans.

## Security
All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). SOC 2 Type II audited annually.
